# FrontierCut v1.13.0 — Microsoft VibeVoice Integration & Live Stream Closed Captions

FrontierCut **v1.13.0** completely unifies on-device speech processing by integrating **Microsoft VibeVoice**, powering multi-speaker conversational dialogue synthesis, single-pass diarized ASR, and real-time RTMP live streaming captions with active speaker badges.

---

### Highlights

* **🎙️ Multi-Speaker Voiceover Studio**: Dedicated "Voice" sidebar panel to write, style, and synthesize multi-character podcast dialogue with distinct acoustic timbres and emotional inflection, auto-assembling into separate audio tracks (`A · Alice`, `A · Bob`) with synced captions and automatic music ducking.
* **🏷️ Single-Pass Diarized ASR**: Transcribe multi-speaker audio up to 60+ minutes with color-coded speaker tags, enabling speaker clip filtering and text-based video cuts.
* **📡 Real-Time Live Streaming Captions**: Burns live closed captions and speaker badges (`Host`, `Guest`) directly into outgoing RTMP broadcast streams at ultra-low latency.
* **🌐 Multilingual Dubbing & Audio Ducking**: 1-click translated dialogue re-voicing and automatic background music ducking under spoken dialogue.
* **🤖 Expanded MCP Tool Surface**: New autonomous tools `generate_conversational_voiceover`, `transcribe_with_diarization`, `dub_timeline_audio`, and `cut_by_speaker`.
